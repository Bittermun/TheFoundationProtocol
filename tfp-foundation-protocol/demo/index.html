THE IDEA BEHIND FOUNDATION

Knowledge should travel.

Contributed work, shared resources, and content that reaches further across community networks.

01

Contribute useful work

The goal is for devices, including modest computers, to perform affordable jobs that improve shared resources. The current task pool is a component; useful lexicon construction remains to be demonstrated.

02

Reuse a shared lexicon

Versioned dictionaries, priors, and adapters should reduce repeated work for other devices and projects. That benefit must be measured, including the cost of building and distributing the resource.

03

Let demand direct work

The intended network allocates preparation and delivery as demand changes, including video, and connects independently operated communities. End-to-end demand scaling and federation remain future work.

What this demo shows today

This local library demonstrates publishing, discovery, exact content retrieval, and local credit accounting. The complete contributed-work and shared-resource cycle is not yet demonstrated.

This is an open research project, built with AI assistance. A successful local demo does not establish censorship resistance, production security, or performance at scale. The custom erasure codec is experimental; it is not an RFC 6330 RaptorQ implementation.
